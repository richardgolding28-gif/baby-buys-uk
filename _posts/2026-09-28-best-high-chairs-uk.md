- question: When can my baby start using a high chair?
  answer: Generally from around 6 months when they can sit up with good head and neck control, which usually lines up with starting weaning. Some chairs offer a reclined newborn setting for earlier use, but always check the specific chair's guidance.

  answer: A reclining option is only really necessary if you want to use the chair from a younger age or for a baby who needs extra support. Once a baby can sit independently, upright with a good footrest is all that matters.

A high chair that's hard to wipe clean or doesn't fold away becomes a daily irritation fast. Here's what to check, and which ones hold up to real use.

## What actually matters

- **Genuinely easy-clean surfaces** — smooth, wipeable seat material without deep seams food can hide in.
- **An adjustable footrest** — matters more than it sounds for comfort and stability once babies start actively eating rather than just being fed.
- **Fold-flat storage** if kitchen space is tight — not every chair folds compactly, worth checking dimensions folded, not just assembled.
- **A genuinely secure harness** — a 5-point harness is worth prioritising over a 3-point one once babies start trying to stand or twist during meals.
